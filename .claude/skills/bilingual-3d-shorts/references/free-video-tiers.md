# Free image-to-video options for hero shots (manual, $0)

Free allowances change often – treat numbers as approximate and check the tool's own page. Always use
**image-to-video with the keyframe as the start frame**, 9:16, ~5s, sound off. Save as `clips/<SHOT>.mp4`.

| Tool | Where | Free allowance (approx.) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Gemini app / Google Flow (Veo) | gemini.google.com, labs.google/flow | small free daily/monthly video quota on free accounts in many regions; more with Google AI plans | Best realism and physics. Upload the keyframe, paste prompt. |
| Kling AI | klingai.com | daily login credits (a few clips/day) | Strong character consistency; queue can be slow on free tier. |
| Meta AI | meta.ai | free "animate" on images | Quick; shorter clips; check 9:16. |
| Hailuo (MiniMax) | hailuoai.video | daily free credits | Good motion; watermark on some free outputs – crop/avoid if present. |
| Pika / Vidu / PixVerse | their sites | small daily credits | Backups when others are exhausted. |

Tips
- Spread hero shots across tools/days; the hook shot (S01) and the twist shot matter most.
- If a tool watermarks a corner, choose another tool – the 2.5D fallback looks cleaner than a watermark.
- 5s clips are trimmed to the shot length automatically; a 3s shot uses the first 3s.
- Any downloaded format works (mp4/mov/webm); any resolution (it is scaled/cropped to 1080x1920).
- Check each tool's terms for commercial use before monetizing.
